Meet Dr. Kerri Thiel

Dr. Thiel grew up on a small farm in Ohio and spent her youth with her dogs, cats, and horses. Going to The Ohio State University College of Veterinary Medicine was a natural choice. After graduating, she spent eight years working as a traditional veterinarian, including owning her own practice.

While Dr. Thiel enjoyed the work immensely, she found it frustrating to come across cases that felt hopeless. In 2015, alternative healing modalities such as spinal manipulation (chiropractic) and Traditional Chinese Medicine were brought to her attention. She trained through Chi University (formerly the Chi Institute) and quickly saw how powerful an integrative approach can be.

Since learning these techniques, she has had the opportunity to improve the health and wellbeing of many dogs and cats, and she looks forward to helping patients regain "Pure Balance" every day.

A New Experience for Timeless Care

Initial visits: Please allow 60–90 minutes. We'll discuss concerns and medical history, then perform both a Traditional Chinese evaluation and a Western exam. A treatment plan is then formulated and performed.

If you have medical records or lab work, please email them to at least 48 hours before your appointment.

Follow-up visits: Please allow 30 minutes. Follow-ups include an abbreviated update on changes and continued treatment. If you have new concerns, please let us know prior to your appointment so we can schedule appropriate time. Additional charges may apply.
