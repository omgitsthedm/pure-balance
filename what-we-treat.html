What alternative medicine can be used for

Every patient is different, and our goal is always the same: improve quality of life and help your animal move, feel, and perform better. Holistic care can be used on its own or alongside traditional veterinary medicine, with plans customized to the condition, severity, and how your animal responds.

Wellness

Performance enhancement and injury/disease prevention.

Musculoskeletal disease

Arthritis, tendon/ligament injury, back or muscle soreness.

Neurological disease

IVDD (disc disease), paralysis/weakness, incontinence, seizures, vestibular disease.

Skin disease

Allergies, masses, lick granulomas.

Metabolic conditions

Cushing's, diabetes, thyroid disease.

Gastrointestinal disease

Diarrhea, IBD, acid reflux, constipation, pancreatitis.

Immune mediated disease

Polyarthritis; blood disease (anemia/thrombocytopenia).

More areas

Respiratory disease, behavioral problems, cancer/benign masses.

How often will my animal need treatment? Treatment intervals depend on what is being treated and the severity of the condition. Typically patients receive an initial 3 treatments administered every 1–2 weeks. After these initial treatments, an appropriate interval will be determined based on the condition being treated. These intervals vary from twice a month to twice a year.

Therapies We May Include in Your Plan

Holistic care works best when modalities are matched to your animal's goals and how they respond. Below are core therapies we commonly combine for an integrative treatment plan.

Acupuncture Options

Depending on your animal's comfort and goals, we may use needle, electrical stimulation, laser, or warming techniques.

  • Dry needle
  • Electroacupuncture
  • Aquapuncture
  • Hemopuncture
  • Laserpuncture
  • Moxabustion

What a TCVM Exam Looks At

A Traditional Chinese Veterinary Medical (TCVM) exam can include tongue and pulse diagnosis, temperature patterns, and sensitivities at specific acupoints to guide point selection and herbal recommendations.

  • Tongue & pulse
  • Temperature patterns
  • Acupoint sensitivity
  • Pattern diagnosis

Veterinary Acupuncture

A gentle, adaptable modality that can support pain control, mobility, neurologic function, digestion, immune balance, and overall wellbeing. Options include dry needle, electroacupuncture, laserpuncture, and moxa when appropriate.

  • Dry needle
  • Electro
  • Laserpuncture
  • Moxa

Spinal Manipulation

Targeted chiropractic-style adjustments to restore normal motion, support the nervous system, and improve comfort. Often helpful for stiffness, compensation patterns, performance support, and recovery.

  • Mobility
  • Comfort
  • Performance
  • Nervous system support

Laser Therapy

Non-invasive light therapy that can reduce inflammation and support tissue repair. Commonly used for arthritis, soft tissue strain, post-procedure comfort, and acute injury support.

  • Inflammation
  • Pain relief
  • Tissue repair
  • Post-injury support

Chinese Herbal Medicine

Formulas selected from a TCVM diagnosis to support long-term balance. Herbal plans can evolve as your animal improves, with practical guidance for dosing and follow-through.

  • TCVM pattern-based
  • Custom formulas
  • Adjust as needed
  • Long-term balance

Food Therapy

Individualized food recommendations that support the body's needs, sensitivities, and goals. We focus on realistic, step-by-step changes that you can maintain.

  • Realistic changes
  • Sensitive stomach support
  • Weight & vitality
  • Whole-body wellness
