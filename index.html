About Pure Balance Holistic Veterinary Services

Pure Balance Holistic Veterinary Services provides integrative veterinary care for dogs and cats in Phoenix, AZ. Dr. Kerri Thiel, DVM, CVA, CVMMP combines Western veterinary medicine with Traditional Chinese Veterinary Medicine (TCVM) to create individualized treatment plans.

Therapies may include veterinary acupuncture (dry needle, electroacupuncture, aquapuncture, hemopuncture, laserpuncture, moxibustion), spinal manipulation (chiropractic adjustment), laser therapy, Chinese herbal medicine, and food therapy.

Conditions commonly supported include arthritis, disc disease, neurological conditions, skin allergies, gastrointestinal issues, metabolic disease, and general wellness for dogs and cats.

Our office is located at 3240 E Union Hills Dr. Suite 133, Phoenix, AZ 85050. Appointments are by email only. To book, email purebalancevet@gmail.com.
